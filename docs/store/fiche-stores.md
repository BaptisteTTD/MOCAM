## Notes pour la vérification Apple (App Review)

À coller dans *App Review Information > Notes*. Sans elles, le testeur d'Apple ne pourra pas utiliser l'app et risque de la refuser.

> MOCAM is the camera companion of MOCAM Studio, a free Windows application (download: https://github.com/BaptisteTTD/MOCAM/releases/latest). The phone connects to MOCAM Studio over the local Wi-Fi by scanning the QR code it displays; the Studio then starts and stops recording on every phone and receives the videos to compute the 3D animation.
> Without a PC, the app opens on the QR code scanner. A demo video of the full workflow is available here: [LIEN VERS UNE VIDÉO DE DÉMONSTRATION].
> Camera: records the actor. Microphone: an audio chirp synchronises the phones. Local network: connection to MOCAM Studio on the user's PC. No account, no data is sent to any server of ours.
